DAN: And there's the friction. Typing in card details to read one article? Nobody does that.
SAM: Exactly. So two things have to be true. The payment has to be almost free to send, and it has to take one click.
DAN: And that's where blockchains come in. On a fast network like Solana, sending a payment costs a tiny fraction of a cent and settles in seconds.
SAM: Plus, with a wallet already connected, paying really is one click. You approve, it's done.

DAN: So how do you reduce that?
SAM: A few ideas. Show a generous teaser, so readers know what they're buying. Keep the price fixed and low, so it becomes a habit rather than a calculation. And maybe offer a daily cap, where you never pay more than a certain amount per day.
DAN: I like the cap. It feels like a subscription, but you only pay for what you use.
SAM: And there's another benefit for writers. With direct payments, the money goes straight to the person who wrote the piece.
